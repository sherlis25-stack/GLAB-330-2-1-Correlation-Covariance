# GLAB 330.2.1 – Correlation Coefficient and Covariance

## Project Overview
This project examines the relationship between temperature and ice cream sales using covariance, variance, standard deviation, and the correlation coefficient.

## Dataset

| Day | Temperature (X) | Ice Cream Sales (Y) |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | 10 | 120 |
| Day 2 | 39 | 110 |
| Day 3 | 15 | 70 |

## Statistical Results
- Mean Temperature: 21.33
- Mean Ice Cream Sales: 100
- Covariance: 46.67
- Temperature Variance: 160.22
- Ice Cream Sales Variance: 466.67
- Temperature Standard Deviation: 12.66
- Ice Cream Sales Standard Deviation: 21.60
- Correlation Coefficient: +0.171

## Conclusion
The correlation coefficient of +0.171 indicates a weak positive linear relationship between temperature and ice cream sales. Temperature alone does not strongly explain changes in ice cream sales in this three-day dataset.
